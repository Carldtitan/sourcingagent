## Direction contract

THESIS: A negotiation is two forces in balance, so this surface renders sourcing as a load-bearing structure where our ceiling pulls against a supplier's floor and every offer sits at the point the structure settles. It refuses the procurement dashboard's arrangement of summary tiles above a sortable table, and it refuses the card grid entirely.

OWN-WORLD: Paper-ash ground (#fcfbf9) with carbon ink (#171614), tension red (#c4402f) carrying every live force and active state, slack ash (#9a9691) for members under no load, and Aonic sand (#c9b48a) reserved for a settled agreement. A named translation of the source world: its concrete-pale ground becomes Aonic's pinned off-white, and its carbon-fibre and nylon-cord materials become drawn line weight rather than photographic texture, because no Aonic asset exists. Components are hairline-ruled blocks with a clipped corner, annotations ride leader lines out to node dots, values set in tabular mono with an explicit sign, and the primary action is a carbon slab with a red trace running through it. One condensed grotesque for display, one mono for every label and figure, both self-hosted.

STORY: The buyer sees a structure under load, reads which members are stressed and which have gone slack, and understands within seconds where a run is holding and where it is about to fail. They act at the two nodes that carry the whole structure: approving who gets contacted, and approving what gets agreed.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A left rail of stations runs the full height in mono caps, current station in red. The centre is the force field: each qualified supplier a strut, drawn at an angle set by its normalised price, joined by red cords whose thickness reads as negotiation pressure and whose colour drops to ash when a supplier goes slack or void. Leader lines run out to node blocks on the right holding the signed values, price delta, lead time and COA verdict, each with its distance from the limit. The brief sits as a ruled head block across the top. The primary action, the approval that releases the run, sits bottom right as the carbon slab, and it is the only filled element on the page.

FORM: Breathing Column, a tensegrity structure of carbon rods and red cord, dealt as challenger 4 in the bolder re-roll and chosen by the user over the deal leader. Seed key 24e3e00f, re-roll round 1, bolder register. Raised before build by a named donation from Command Wall: every measured value renders as a load against its limit with the remaining margin stated, which gives the structure the vocabulary for laboratory certificate data it lacked on its own.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
